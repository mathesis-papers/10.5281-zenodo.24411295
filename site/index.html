Mathar's inverse-quintic recurrence conjecture and extensions of Barry's cubic formulas

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

We prove R. J. Mathar's 'Conjecture D-finite with recurrence' in the third FORMULA line of OEIS A392976, revision 19 (January 31, 2026; conjecture dated January 29, 2026), and extend the cubic coefficient identities proved by Barry (2011, Theorem 3 and Example 7). The coefficients of the formal inverse of t((1-t^2)^2-t), divided by x, satisfy the stated six-term recurrence at every integer n >= 5. A change of variable reduces the assertion to an explicit polynomial identity. The same argument gives a nonzero homogeneous differential equation of order five; a trace argument lowers the existence bound to order four. Lagrange inversion gives an integer-exponent family containing the entry's binomial-sum formula and its three cross-referenced sequences, as well as all positive integral powers of their generating functions. Further corollaries cover the cubic entry's alternate sum, differential recurrence and derivative formulas.
